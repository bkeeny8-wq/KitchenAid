 Turkey Gravy
 Made from the vegetable bed and drippings in the roasting pan. Makes about 4 cups.


 Ingredients
    Pan base                 all the roasted vegetables and drippings from the bottom of the roasting
                             pan
    Fat                      1/4 cup (4 tbsp) skimmed turkey fat from the drippings, topped up with
                             UNSALTED butter if short
    Flour                    1/4 cup all-purpose
    Stock                    4 cups UNSALTED turkey or chicken stock, warmed
    Pepper                   freshly ground, to taste

 Note: the butter and stock are both unsalted, so nothing in here is salted. Taste at the very end and decide for
 yourself before adding anything.

 Instructions
 1. After the turkey comes out to rest, pour the vegetables and drippings from the roasting pan into a
     bowl or fat separator. Scrape up the browned bits from the pan and add them too.
 2. Skim the fat off the top and set aside. Keep the dark juices with the vegetables.
 3. Warm the stock in a saucepan so it goes into the gravy hot.
 4. In a large saucepan over medium heat, add 1/4 cup of the skimmed fat, topped up with unsalted
     butter if you are short. Whisk in the flour and cook, whisking, for 2 minutes until it smells toasty.
 5. Slowly whisk in the warm stock, then the juices from the roasting pan. Keep whisking until smooth.
 6. Simmer 10 to 15 minutes, stirring often, until the gravy coats the back of a spoon.
 7. Press the roasted vegetables through a fine strainer into the gravy, squeezing the roasted garlic out
     of its skins and scraping the underside of the strainer. Discard the leftover solids.
 8. Add pepper, then taste. Thin with a splash of stock if too thick, or simmer a few more minutes if too
     thin.
 9. Keep warm over low heat and serve with the turkey.

 Tip: for a smoother gravy, blend the vegetables with a ladle of the gravy using an immersion blender, then stir
 it all back in.
