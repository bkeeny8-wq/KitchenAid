 Roast Turkey
 Herb butter turkey on a bed of vegetables. 350 F, 13 minutes per pound.


                                       DO. NOT. SALT.
                                 DO NOT USE SALTED BUTTER.
 Shopping List
    Butter                   2 sticks, UNSALTED
    Pepper                   lots, best if freshly ground, with larger portions of peppercorns
    Fresh thyme              1 packet
    Lemon                    1
    White onion              1 for the cavity. Yellow can be a backup. Do not use red.
    Vegetable bed            onion, leek, garlic, carrots, and celery (see below)

 Bed of Vegetables
 The bottom of the roasting pan is a bed of onion, leek, garlic, carrots, and celery. The turkey sits on
 top of it. Save the vegetables and drippings, they are the base for the gravy (separate page).

    Onion                    2, cut into large pieces
    Leek                     2, cleaned well and cut into large pieces
    Garlic                   1 whole head, cut in half
    Carrots                  4, cut into large chunks
    Celery                   4 stalks, cut into large pieces

 Note: these amounts are a starting point. Scale them to fill the bottom of your pan. Do not salt the vegetables
 either.

 Butter Mixture
 1.5 to 2 sticks of softened UNSALTED butter. Add lots of pepper and 3/4 of a pack of fresh thyme and
 mix well. It should be well speckled.
 Spread onto the entire turkey. Under the skin on the breasts will yield the best result.

 In the Cavity
 Slice the lemon in half and push one half in first. Cut large chunks off a quartered onion. Do not chop
 fine, you want big pieces, and insert as much as you can. Add the remaining sprigs of thyme and close
 up the end with the other half of the lemon.

 Instructions
 1. Make sure the turkey is fully thawed. Heat the oven to 350 F.
 2. Make the bed: spread the onion, leek, garlic, carrots, and celery over the bottom of the roasting pan.
 3. Make the butter mixture: mix the softened UNSALTED butter with lots of pepper and 3/4 of the fresh
     thyme until well speckled.
 4. Spread the butter mixture over the entire turkey, getting it under the skin on the breasts for the best
     result.
 5. Fill the cavity: lemon half first, big chunks of onion packed in as tightly as you can, the remaining
     thyme sprigs, then close up the end with the other lemon half.
 6. Set the turkey on the bed of vegetables. Do not salt anything.
 7. Cover and roast at 350 F for the first 2/3 of the total time (13 minutes per pound).
 8. Uncover and roast for the last 1/3 of the time, until golden.
 9. Check that the thickest part of the thigh reads 165 F. Ovens vary, so start checking a little before the
     time is up.
 10. Move the turkey to a board to rest. Keep the vegetables and drippings in the pan for the gravy
     (separate page).

 Cooking Times
 13 minutes per pound at 350 F. Covered for the first 2/3, uncovered for the last 1/3.

    Turkey weight      Total time                   Covered (first 2/3)           Uncovered (last 1/3)
    10 lb              2 hr 10 min                  1 hr 27 min                   43 min
    12 lb              2 hr 36 min                  1 hr 44 min                   52 min
    14 lb              3 hr 02 min                  2 hr 01 min                   1 hr 01 min
    16 lb              3 hr 28 min                  2 hr 19 min                   1 hr 09 min
    18 lb              3 hr 54 min                  2 hr 36 min                   1 hr 18 min
