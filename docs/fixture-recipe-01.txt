NY Strip Crostini with Creamed Horseradish Spinach
Crisp baguette, warm creamed horseradish spinach, and thin slices of seared NY strip.

   Makes                                   Active time                Total time
   12 crostini (serves 4)                  About 35 minutes           About 1 hour 10 minutes


Ingredients
Crostini and steak
       12 slices            baguette, 1/2 inch thick
       3 tbsp               olive oil
       16 oz                NY strip steak, about 1.25 inches thick
       1 tsp                kosher salt
       1/2 tsp              black pepper
       1 tbsp               butter
       2                    garlic cloves, smashed

Creamed horseradish spinach
       10 oz                baby spinach
       1                    shallot, minced
       1 tbsp               butter
       4 oz                 cream cheese
       3 tbsp               heavy cream
       3 tbsp               prepared horseradish, drained
       2 tbsp               grated Parmesan
       1 tsp                lemon juice
       1/4 tsp              kosher salt

To finish
       1 tbsp               fresh chives, finely chopped
       Pinch                flaky salt

Instructions
  1.   Season the steak (30 min). Pat the steak dry and season all over with 1 tsp kosher salt and
       1/2 tsp black pepper. Let it sit at room temperature so it sears evenly.

  2.   Toast the baguette (9 min). Heat the oven to 400F. Arrange the baguette slices on a sheet
       pan, brush with olive oil, and bake until golden and crisp.
  3.   Sear the steak (7 to 8 min). Heat a heavy skillet over high heat until smoking. Sear the steak 3
       to 4 minutes per side for medium rare (125F). In the last minute, add 1 tbsp butter and the
       smashed garlic and spoon the butter over the steak.

  4.   Rest the steak (9 min). Move the steak to a board and let it rest. Wipe most of the drippings
       out of the skillet before the spinach.

  5.   Wilt the spinach (5 min). In a medium pan over medium heat, melt 1 tbsp butter and cook the
       shallot for 2 minutes. Add the spinach in handfuls until wilted. Transfer to a clean towel or
       sieve, squeeze out as much liquid as you can, and roughly chop.

  6.   Make the creamed spinach (2 min). In the same pan over low heat, melt the cream cheese
       with the heavy cream and horseradish, stirring until smooth. Fold in the spinach, Parmesan,
       lemon juice, and 1/4 tsp salt. Taste and add more horseradish if you want more heat.

  7.   Slice the steak. Slice the steak thinly against the grain, then cut the slices to fit the crostini.

  8.   Assemble. Spoon warm spinach onto each crostini, top with a slice of steak, and finish with
       chives and a pinch of flaky salt. Serve right away.


Notes
• The spinach can be made a day ahead and gently rewarmed with a splash of cream.
• Squeezing the spinach dry is the key step. Extra water makes the crostini soggy.
• For a sharper bite, stir a little extra horseradish into the spinach just before serving, or add a thin
horseradish drizzle on top of the steak.
• Toast the baguette just before serving so it stays crisp.
